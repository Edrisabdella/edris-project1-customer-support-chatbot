# Online Shop FAQ

## Orders and Order Status
Customers can check their order status from the Orders section of their account. The FAQ does not provide additional order-status troubleshooting instructions.

## Shipping and Delivery
Shipping and delivery information is provided through the customer's order details. Specific delivery estimates may vary by order. If the FAQ does not answer a delivery question, contact human support.

## Returns and Refunds
Returns and refunds are handled according to the shop's return policy shown in the customer's order/account information. If a specific return or refund situation is not covered here, contact human support.

## Payments
The shop supports the payment methods presented during checkout. If a payment issue is caused by a technical malfunction, treat it as a bug report. Questions about normal payment functionality should be answered only from information available in this FAQ.

## Products
Product availability and product-specific information can vary. If the requested information is not provided by the FAQ, contact human support.

## Accounts
Customers can use the account area to manage their available account information. Questions not answered by this FAQ should be escalated to human support.

## Privacy and Policies
For privacy or policy questions not explicitly answered by this FAQ, contact human support.

## Support
When the FAQ does not contain enough information to answer a platform question, the customer should be directed to human support.
